## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a private mark from a notebook or a small zine, not a perfect logo. Use a strong outer contour, a few identity features, clumsy proportion and a line that hesitates, repeats and stays a little unfinished. Flat spot color, paper grain and print error. One focus, very small, in a large breath of paper. Reject commercial geometry, a full scene, smooth vector and over-cleaning.
